RADS-Envelope

LI-RADS reports that never contradict the rules, and say exactly what is still uncertain.

Try it See an example

The problem

  • A report can describe the findings of one LI-RADS category and state another.
  • Language models write the category as free text, and nothing checks it against the findings.
  • Asked directly, the same local model contradicted its own findings in a measurable share of reports.

How it works

  1. Paste the reportOr drop a .txt or .docx file. Each liver observation is read separately, with the words behind every finding.
  2. Rules decide the categoryThe published ACR LI-RADS table computes the category. The impression is written from that result, so the two always agree.
  3. See what is certain, what is notEvery category the findings could still support, and the one question that would settle it.

Who it is for

Radiologists
Check a draft before signing: the category, its evidence and what could change it.
Residents
See how each finding moves the category, step by step through the table.
Quality leads
Audit a batch of reports for impressions that disagree with their own findings.
Tumour boards and hepatology
Know which observations are settled and which need one more answer.

What makes it different

Never contradicts the rulesThe category is computed from the findings, not written by the model.
Certainty EnvelopeHedged words, missing features and sizes near a cut-off become an explicit range of categories.
Alerts only when management changesUncertainty that cannot change what happens to the patient is shown, not alarmed.
Runs fully offlineOne laptop GPU and a local model. Nothing is sent to an external service.

Runs on one laptop. No report leaves the machine.
